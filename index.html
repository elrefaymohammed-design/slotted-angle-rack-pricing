Rack configuration

Dimensions in mm
Common sizes: 500–1200 mm in 50 mm steps. Manual entry for special width.
Upright spec: 4 uprights per bay, 40 × 40 × 1.5 mm ST37 steel, 76 mm flat pattern. Uprights are priced per upright.
SA shelf spec: flat pattern = bay width + 90 mm × bay depth + 90 mm. Shelf thickness choices: 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0 mm.
02

Connection & reinforcement

Slotted-web connection: 8 webs at each web level per bay. Webs sit at the first and last shelf levels, and a middle level is added for heights of 2,500 mm or more. Each web level uses 24 M8 × 20 sets; other levels use 8 sets.
03

Euro-hook bracing

Bracing spec: galvanized 39 × 39 × 2 mm strips nested from galvanized 1,000 × 2,000 × 2 mm sheets. Side-brace length = sqrt((bay depth − 28)^2 + 900^2) + 40 mm. Back-brace length = sqrt((bay width − 28)^2 + 900^2) + 40 mm. Both side and back X-bracing counts are calculated as ceil(height / 900) per bay, and each X uses 2 strips + 5 M6 × 12 sets.

Optional shelf dividers

Separator rule: front and back separators each use 1 quantity per selected shelf, side separators use 2 quantity per selected shelf, and each separator has a 55 mm flat-pattern width. Front/back length = bay width. Side length = bay depth.
